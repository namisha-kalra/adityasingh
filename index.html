About me

Journalism training, field experience, and a youth-first view of politics.

Aditya Singh is a new-age Indian political strategist who merges the analytical lens of a journalist with the precision of a campaign architect. As Co-Founder and Director of V SCAN Alliance (Vikas Strategic Citizens Accountability Network), he leads a youth-driven movement for transparency, accountability and people-first politics.

With over 3 years of on-ground experience, he has shaped narratives and data-led digital campaigns for parties and candidates across India. His strength is strategy that not only trends online but resonates offline — media insight, social influence and grassroots engagement in one formula.

A postgraduate in Journalism from Delhi University, Aditya brings academic rigour to every campaign. Before co-founding V SCAN Alliance, he worked with leading political consulting firms on public outreach, content strategy and influencer-driven political storytelling.
